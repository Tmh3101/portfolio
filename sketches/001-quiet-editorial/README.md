## Variant 001 — Quiet Editorial

### Design stance
Single-column, type-forward layout with maximal whitespace. Hierarchy is carried by
Space Grotesk display sizes and generous vertical rhythm, not by boxes or color.
Closest reference: Notion / editorial portfolio.

### Key choices
- Layout: one centered 1080px column, sections split only by hairline dividers.
- Typography: very large hero (clamp up to 4rem), body capped at ~52ch for readability.
- Color: pure monochrome, ink-on-paper, no accent hue.
- Interaction: only a theme toggle + real link hovers; no motion beyond opacity.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: calm, readable, content-first, feels authored not templated.
- Weak at: less "product/tool" feel; work list can feel sparse on wide screens.

### Best for
A reader who wants to understand the person and their work through words, not dashboards.
